## Criteria (frozen 2026-11-03, Gate 0)
- Success (the card's definition of done):
  1. Five real/generated pairs with aligned depth and divergence heat.
  2. The scrubber and the overlay work at 390 px.
  3. The film and the essay are published.
  4. The case-study "Done means" on top: clearance passed, spend logged, STATUS and the planner agree, the plain-words layer is live and its real-reader test is logged, and the CV bullets are written with `check_cv.py` passing.
- Kill rules:
  1. *Alignment floor.* The noise floor is mono depth (Depth Anything 3) on the real frames, measured against the real tank. If its median AbsRel_tank is above 25 % on three or more recordings, mono depth cannot read this tank and the heat map is noise. Stop, swap the mono model once (Video Depth Anything or Depth Anything V2 metric), and re-run the floor. If it is still above 25 %, the card ships as a negative result with the floor shown and no claim about the engines.
  2. *Camera motion.* A prompt whose probes move the camera on three of five recordings is rewritten once, in house wording (not a new prompt). A second failure drops that prompt from the comparison and is reported. For the dive prompt D it is read in reverse: a prompt D whose probes don't move the camera on three of five recordings is rewritten once, then dropped and reported.
  3. *Spend.* £30 hard cap. The generation rounds stop at £25 (raised from £22 at Gate 0 to carry prompt N and the fresh disparity), so the page, film and clearance keep about £5. The hunt runs last, so if the cap binds, the hunt's take count shrinks, and the page says how many takes it had.
  4. *Self-test.* Render the reconstruction along a known straight path (200 su forward, in uncorrected stereo units, over 48 frames, with ±3° of yaw), run the depth model and the registration on the renders, and compare. If the recovered path's endpoint error exceeds 15 % of the travel on three or more recordings, path recovery can't be trusted. The dives are then reported with heat at frame 0 and the verified share only, and the path claims are dropped.
- Budget: 8 days, £30 (generation rounds capped at £25).

### Design choices frozen with the criteria
- The real depth is computed fresh: an off-the-shelf stereo matcher (Stereo Anywhere) runs on the published rectified pairs, uncorrected for the tank's flat window. Distances are in uncorrected stereo units. OpenCV SGBM is the fallback.
- Alignment uses one scale and one shift per clip, fitted on frame 0 and frozen for the clip, with a per-frame refit as the secondary view. The page's first screen says that the alignment is a scale-and-shift fit and that the stereo ruler is uncorrected for the tank's flat window.
- There are three prompts, frozen in `pipeline/prompts.py` before the probes: D (the dive), B (the open continuation) and N (a nudged continuation, run against B with matched seeds). With five recordings a side, the B-against-N comparison is a description, not a significance test. The invention hunt uses B word for word.
- No new recordings: only the published dataset, its published labels and off-the-shelf public models.
